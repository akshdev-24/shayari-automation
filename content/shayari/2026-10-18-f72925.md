Raat ne chhupa andhera, par teri yaad ne diya roshni ka saaza
Har do jhonke mein dhundhti hai, tu hi hai woh ilm ka raaza
Tum naam tera, dil ki dhadkan, subah tak badal ke jaaye wala
Chand ki khoj mein, tu hai hi safar ki hawa, mitti se utre hue phool jaise
