Kadam badha, raah khud banegi, josh se bhara hai har saans
Manzil tak pehle khud ko jeeto, safar hi hai zindagi ka imaan
Dhadkon ki awaaz sun, hawa ke saath aage badh
Zindagi ka rang hai josh, manzil mile jab tak na rukna kabhi
