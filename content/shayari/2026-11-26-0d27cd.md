Manzil door sahi, par hauslon mein aag hai,
Tere intezaar mein chalna, yehi mera raaz hai.
Raste poochte hain mera naam, main kehta hoon 'Ishq',
Tere bina har kadam be-maqsad, tere saath har manzil aaj hai.
