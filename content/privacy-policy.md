---

Ye site aapse koi personal jaankari nahi maangti.

**Cookies aur vigyapan.** Hum Google AdSense jaise third-party vigyapan dene wale use kar sakte hain. Google aur uske partners aapki pichli visits ke aadhar par vigyapan dikhane ke liye cookies (jaise DoubleClick cookie) use kar sakte hain. Aap Google ke Ads Settings se personalised ads band kar sakte hain.

**Analytics.** Site ki traffic samajhne ke liye hum anonymous analytics use kar sakte hain.

**Links.** Hamari site par dusri websites ke links ho sakte hain. Unki privacy policy par hamara control nahi hai.

Is policy mein badlav hone par ye page update kiya jayega.
