---
title: "Baarish Ka Sukoon"
date: 2026-10-04T10:01:15Z
category: "intezaar"
mood: "sukoon wala"
keyword: "baarish"
---

Baarish ki ek boond ne sardi bhari raat ko,
Chhoo liya intezar ka wo sukoon jo lamha tha,
Tera naam bheega hawa mein bhi khushboo ban ke,
Ab baarish bhi yaadein bhoolane ka sabab ban gayi.
