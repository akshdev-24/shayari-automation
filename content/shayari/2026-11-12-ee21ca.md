Barishon ke andhere mein tera hasina kho gaya,
Dil ki raunak ban ke reh gaya, bas teri yaad ka dhua.
Tere bin mera woh dhuaan bhi andhere bhar jayega,
Mitti ki boondon mein tumhare bin, jeena humne chhoda.
