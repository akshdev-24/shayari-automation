---

Hawa bhi ruk jaati hai dekh kar hausla,
Zindagi ko apni mutthi mein kar lena seekho.
Jo tham gayi wo hawa hai, jo chal pade wo toofan hai,
Apni raah khud bana, yehi toh asli imtihaan hai.
