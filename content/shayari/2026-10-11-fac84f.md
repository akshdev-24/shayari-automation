---
title: "Dil ke Tanhai ka Sauthi"
date: 2026-10-11T10:15:25Z
category: "intezaar"
mood: "shikayat bhara"

---

Shahar ki hawa mein bhar do, har dil ki dhadkan ki awaaz
Safar bhar tum bhi the, ab tanhai bhi mujhpe hai—zindagi ki sada bhul
Dil hamesha khwab mein rehta hai, khamoshiyon ko teri yaad ki dhora
