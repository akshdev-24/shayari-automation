---

Subah ki hawa mein diya jalta, dosti ki dhadkan udaas si
Khwabon mein bhi teri yaad ka dard, hawa ne chhoda sirf khamoshi
Raat ke saaye mein, diya ki roshni se guzarti, dosti ka rishta khamoshi se bolta
