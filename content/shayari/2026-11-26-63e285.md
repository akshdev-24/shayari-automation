---
title: "Raat Se Hawa"
date: 2026-11-26T13:02:26Z
category: "yaadein"
mood: "sukoon wala"
keyword: "hawa"
---

Hawa ne sapne ko dhoom di, teri rang ko batata hi aur kapta mein bhi dard dikhta hai.
Chand ki chhani mein yaad ki dard toot jati hai, par hawa ne har roop se sukoon lai diya.
Barish ki raat ne khada barkao, lekin main hawa pe bana rahi aur samajhne mein bhool paadi rehta hai.
