---

Baarish aati hai toh sirf mitti ko mehakti hai,
Mujhe toh bas teri yaadon ki keechad me hi dhakel jaati hai.
Asmaan roya toh saari duniya bheeg gayi,
Mere andar ka sukha dariya kisi ko nazar na aaya.
