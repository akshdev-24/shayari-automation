---
title: "Hawa ne yadon ko udai"
date: 2026-10-01T11:46:56Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "hawa"
---

Hawa ke parde mein, dost ka dard samjha jaaya yad aaya
Tera mera saath bina, raatein hawa si ho gayi
Raat ki hawa ke saath, teri yaad tum se karti hai shikayat
Dosti ki yeh duniya, agar tu na hota to hawa bhi ajnabi lagti
