Subah ki chai ki pyali mein, dil ki baat khushboo Se milti, josh se bhare dil
Har sip mein bhar aata hai zindagi ka rang, koi dard na rahe
Chai ki garamiyon mein, sapno ki uddan, jeevan ki naya shuru
