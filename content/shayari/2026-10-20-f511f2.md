---
title: "khwabon ka sawaal"
date: 2026-10-20T08:49:53Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "khwab"
---

Har khwab mein tere saaye aate hain, par dosti ka sawaal bhi unhi mein jhoota pad jaaye
Tu sochhta hai khwabon se dur, par har dard bhi tera hi haath leaaye
Khwab toh bas ek pal ka hota hai, par dosti ka intezaar saal bhar rahaaye
