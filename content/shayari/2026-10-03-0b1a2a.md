Safar mein tujhko yaad karte hue, manzil hamesha sirf ek khwaab hi nahi
 par dard ka raag baja ke apni hi sochniyon ko jeetna sikha
 yeh raste zindagi ke nikle intezaar ke hosh yaad rakhte hue
