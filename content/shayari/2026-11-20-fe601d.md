---
title: "diya ka intezaar"
date: 2026-11-20T12:04:46Z
category: "intezaar"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "diya"
---

Raaton ki kami ne teri khidki par diya bhar diya,
Dil ne bhi roshni bhula bas yeh intezaar chhoda.
Tum aa gaye to chand sookh gaya shikayat ka samna tha,
Diya jala ke andhere ko khamoshi ne phir sambhala.
