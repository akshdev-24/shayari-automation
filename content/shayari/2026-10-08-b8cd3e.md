Chand ki chhani mein bhi, teri yaad ka dard basa hua hai.
Har subah ki roshni se hi, khwahishen siyaah ho kar, ab basu husniyat se guzar rahi hai.
Tere bina zindagi ki har yaad, ek shikayat banke, raaste khudko rok kar sada door lagti hain.
