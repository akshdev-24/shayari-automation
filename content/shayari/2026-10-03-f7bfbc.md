---
title: "Manzil ki talaash"
date: 2026-10-03T09:22:51Z
category: "yaadein"
mood: "udaas"
keyword: "manzil"
---

Manzil itni door ho gayi ke har kadam khudko roke raha hai,
Yadon ka safar abhi purana nahi, lekin har ruka hua pal bole raha hai.
Raaton ki sadaliyaan teri yaad ke jaal bich rahi hain,
Manzil to ek khwaab he raha, lekin har kadam ne khayalon ko jara jara aage badaya.
