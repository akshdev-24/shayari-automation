Hawa ke saath jaati hai teri yaad ki dhoop,
Pattan ke pappad kya haaye, zindagi bhar ke saath.
Dil ujda, daar mein, lagta nahin hai kuchh pehle se.
Safar bhar tum the, ab tanhai bhi mujhpe hai, jee ke mar jayenge.
