Khwabon mein bhi teri yaad ka dard, dil ki dhadkan thandi ho gayi
Raat bhi guzarti, akele bitha hai, har lamha teri yaad ke saath
Subah ki hawa bhi teri khushboo se bhar aayi, par main khamosh hi reh gaya
