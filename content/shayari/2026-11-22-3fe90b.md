Raat jab gher aati hai khamoshi ki chadar odh ke,
Teri yaadon ka dard raat ko bhi bana deta hai bewaqt.
Tumne kaha tha wapas aayega, lekin raat khatam hui tum nahi,
Ab sirf ye aansu hain jo raat ko bheegte hain.
