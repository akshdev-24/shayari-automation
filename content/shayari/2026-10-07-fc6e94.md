---
title: "Chand Aur Judai"
date: 2026-10-07T10:31:05Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "udaas"
keyword: "chand"
---

Wahi chand hai raahon pe, par raaste juda huye hain,
Dosti ka wo waada ab toot kar bikhar gaya hai,
Teri hasti hui yaadon ko ab ye shaam bhi chhupati hai,
Kyunki doori ne milna toh humara hi khatam kar diya hai.
