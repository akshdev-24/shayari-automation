title: "sukoon ka safar"
date: 2026-11-28T12:11:50Z
category: "dosti"
mood: "sukoon wala"

---

safar hum dono chalte hain, raaste hi badalte hain
chupke sukoon milta hai, meri har dhadkan tumse hai
raat ne humara saans rok liya, lekin teri yaad josh ko
sukoon bas hai yeh safar, hum dono isi josh mein
