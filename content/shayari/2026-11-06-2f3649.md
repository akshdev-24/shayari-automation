Tumhari yaad ka ehsaas ab raat ki khamoshi bhi bhula,
Sukoon ki raat hai, bas meri dua hai tere haathon.
Main intezaar karunga, tera intezaar rahegi,
Khamoshion mein bhi dil ki dhadkan hai sukoon ki woh.
