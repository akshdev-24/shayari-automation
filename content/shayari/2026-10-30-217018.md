---
title: "Baarish Ke Nasha"
date: 2026-10-30T10:22:48Z
category: "sad"
mood: "sukoon wala"
keyword: "baarish"
---

Barish ki boondein ghiraa gayi nazar, dil ki dhadkan thodi si sulak.
Subah ki hawa mein tujhko chahte hue dil, khamoshi mein bhi tujhko soak.
Is baarish mein teri yaad ka sukoon, jaise dard se bhi gehri.
