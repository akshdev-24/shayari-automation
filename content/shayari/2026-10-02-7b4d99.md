---
title: "Aaine ki Shikayat"
date: 2026-10-02T09:59:48Z
category: "intezaar"
mood: "shikayat bhara"
keyword: "aaina"
---

Raatein guzarti hain aaine ke saamne baith kar,
Tera aks bhi dikhta hai, bas tu hi nahi aata.
Yeh sheesha bhi ab mujhse khafa sa rehta hai,
Mera intezaar dekhte-dekhte thak gaya hai.
