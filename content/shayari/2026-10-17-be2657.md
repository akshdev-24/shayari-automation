Subah ki roshni mein, khamoshi ki bandooq mein zindagi josh se jalta hai
Har saans mein banती hai naya intezaar, dhadkan mein khoi jaaye mohabbat ki roshni
Tere bina khamoshi, par dil ki arifiyat se bhare, jeevan ki har raah mein rang le aaye
