title: "Dosti Ki Khamoshi"
date: 2026-10-27T09:16:38Z

---

Doston ka sath hamesha, magar khamoshi kitni badi hoti hai
Har phate huye ruxu mein tera naam ek yaad ki kitaab hoti hai
Udaas hawaon mein bhi, dosti ke raaz ek khamoshi hoti hai
Khamoshi mein bhi ek bol bhi hoti hai, jeena ka sab se mushkil sura
