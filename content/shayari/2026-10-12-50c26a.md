Subah ki hawa mein bhar do, har pal naya khwab, intezaar ki sada
Raat ki raunen mein tu aane ki khwahish, dilon ki dhadkanen khwabon se bhari
Hawaon ke jhok mein meri duaa hai, teri yaad ka intezaar, mere sapnon ka saaz
