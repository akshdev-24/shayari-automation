Safar ke raste mein, har kadam par naya sukoon chhupa hota hai
Jab tu saath ho, khamoshi bhi khushiyon ki layak ban jati hai
Is journey ko jeetein, to milta hai zindagi ka har pal ek shaant aashirwaad.
