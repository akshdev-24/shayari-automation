Raat ke saharon mein, tu na raha, meri dhadkanyan sirf khamoshiyon ki baahon mein
Shikayat ka dard, teri yaadon ki baarishon ki tarah, dil ko dhundhla
Subah ki hawa bhi, tujhse khushiyon ki khushboo nahi, bas khamoshiyan ki dhoom
